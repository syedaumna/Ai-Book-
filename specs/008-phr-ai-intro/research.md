**Objective**: Establish a clear, concise, and broadly accepted definition of PHR AI.
**Key Questions**:
-   What are the core components that define PHR AI? (e.g., embodied intelligence, human-robot interaction, physical manipulation, autonomous decision-making).
-   How does PHR AI differentiate itself from broader AI or traditional robotics?
-   What are the interdisciplinary aspects involved (e.g., AI, robotics, neuroscience, cognitive science)?
**Research Method**: Review academic literature (journals, conference papers), leading industry publications, and established definitions from prominent robotics/AI organizations.

## 2. Significance and Impact of PHR AI

**Objective**: Articulate why PHR AI is a critical and transformative field.
**Key Questions**:

**Research Method**: Consult foresight reports, industry analyses, ethical guidelines for AI/robotics, and case studies of successful robotic deployments.
